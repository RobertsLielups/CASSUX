# Game Modes

## Implemented

### Solo Case Training

Five randomized questions per session from 12 Latvian cases. Topics include evidence, causality, decisions, assumptions, and systems. Every correct answer earns 100 match points; solo speed adds nothing. XP is 25 per correct answer, 10 per incorrect answer, plus 20 for completion.

### Solo Premise Training

The same loop over 12 questions about premises, conclusions, necessary/sufficient conditions, hidden assumptions, valid inference, and fallacies.

## Planned

- **Case Duels:** identical verified scenario for both players; answers hidden until both submit or the round resolves.
- **Premise Duels:** shared duel infrastructure focused on argument evaluation.
- **Detective:** Solo Detective, Detective Race, and Detective vs Suspects over immutable case truth, timelines, alibis, evidence, and secrets. Concealment does not imply guilt; competitive eliminations need reasons/evidence.
- **Mafia:** original social deduction with secret roles, discussion, voting, hidden objectives, and post-game reasoning review.
- **Hidden-role sabotage strategy:** original cooperation, trust, resources, and covert disruption; no copied commercial expression.
- **Economic strategy:** original assets, allocation, negotiation, risk, and changing conditions; no Monopoly board, names, or presentation.
- **Systems:** interconnected simulations such as cities, businesses, crises, and transport with plausible delayed consequences.

Large premium games are not playable and are shown only as `Drīzumā`.

## Experimental

**Justify Your Decision** may ask for a reason when accepting/rejecting explicitly eligible in-game proposals. Any possible match penalty needs balancing before Ranked. It must never penalize a correct “No,” refusal of purchases/invitations/communication, unwanted social interaction, withdrawal of consent, or sound strategy merely because it rejects a proposal.

## Content constraints

All modes remain realistic. No supernatural truth mechanisms. Important conclusions depend on verifiable facts. Ranked closed-answer questions must have one defensible answer, and AI-generated content cannot enter ranked pools without validation.
