# Monetization

**Status: planned. No payments, checkout, subscriptions, entitlements, marketplace, or functional purchase UI exists in Milestone 001.**

## Product direction

- **Free:** solo case/premise training, basic 1v1, profile/progression, and one completed trial match in each large premium game.
- **CASSUX Pass:** one simple tier, target approximately €5/month, for full Detective, Mafia, sabotage strategy, and economic strategy access.
- **Cosmetics:** optional Stickman appearance and animation. Some cosmetics are earned through play.

Spending never buys rating, answers/evidence, clock advantages, stronger roles, matchmaking priority that changes fairness, loot boxes, or competitive power.

## Review gates

Real payments require legal/tax/consumer-rights review by supported region, age/privacy review, platform and payment-provider compliance, accessible cancellation, server-side payment verification, signed webhook handling, idempotency, refunds/chargebacks, entitlement reconciliation, fraud controls, and auditable support tooling.

A future cosmetic marketplace is only a possibility. It needs a separate legal, security, payments, identity/age, AML/fraud, pricing-manipulation, and moderation review. Account trafficking, transferred ratings, fabricated player histories, and bot achievements presented as human accomplishments are prohibited.
