# CASSUX Product Specification

## Product intent

CASSUX is a competitive thinking and strategy gaming platform: entertaining first, with analytical reasoning, evidence evaluation, argumentation, decision-making, strategy, and systems thinking developed through play. Its promise is **“Think. Play. Outsmart.”** The experience must feel like a premium game platform, not an educational quiz portal.

Core rules are non-negotiable: skill over spending, no pay-to-win, explicit and consistently enforced rules, authoritative validation of competitive results, realistic worlds, fixed objective truth in investigations, validated ranked content, and no fake human activity.

## Status legend

- **Implemented:** usable in Milestone 001.
- **Planned:** accepted product direction, not functional yet.
- **Experimental:** requires design validation and playtesting.
- **Review required:** needs later legal, security, payment, rights, or safety review.

## Milestone 001 — implemented

- Latvian responsive interface with Home, Play, Learn, Progress, Profile, and Settings.
- Solo Case Training and Solo Premise Training.
- 24 original, reviewed Latvian questions with exactly three choices and one answer key.
- Random five-question sessions, repeat avoidance, answer locking, explanation, scoring, results, restart, and mistake review.
- Local guest profile, XP, level, answer totals, accuracy, completed sessions, and original Stickman SVG.
- Seven concise learning topics.
- Shared future match contracts, tests, and developer documentation.

XP measures participation. It is not an intelligence score. Rating remains a separate future measure of competitive outcomes.

## Platform areas — planned evolution

- **Home:** daily challenge, active matches, progression, recent modes.
- **Play:** solo, live/correspondence 1v1, private/ranked/casual multiplayer, later tournaments.
- **Learn:** reasoning concepts connected to play.
- **Progress:** history, mode ratings, skill performance, mistake review.
- **Profile:** identity, Stickman cosmetics, achievements, statistics, later friends.
- **Settings:** language, accessibility, audio, animation, and account controls.

Initial language is Latvian (`lv`). English is planned. Code, schemas, and technical documents use English.

## Realism and investigation integrity

The world excludes genuine magic, monsters, paranormal explanations, and supernatural evidence. Dreams, imagined scenes, and unreliable memories may exist only as subjective experiences. A dream never proves a crime. Detective cases require a fixed timeline, culprit, evidence model, and logically supported solution that cannot change during a match.

## Progression and identity

- **XP:** participation and activity.
- **Level:** long-term experience derived from XP.
- **Rating:** future server-validated competitive skill, separated by relevant game/time categories.
- **Skill performance:** future topic-level accuracy and review data; never marketed as a scientifically validated intelligence score.

Every player begins with a neutral white Stickman. Earned and optional paid cosmetics may change visuals only, never performance.

## Systems-thinking learning framework — planned

Future content may use system parameters, stocks and flows, buffers, delays, reinforcing/balancing feedback, information flows, rules and incentives, self-organization, goals, paradigms, and assumptions. Questions should distinguish root causes from symptoms and deterministic game-model results from uncertain real-world outcomes.

This outline uses only the conceptual summary supplied for Donella H. Meadows’ 1999 paper *Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System*. The source PDF is not present in this repository, and the project does not claim to have reviewed it. The framework is not a universal answer key.

## Explicitly not implemented

Authentication, cloud persistence, matchmaking, real players, live clocks, correspondence deadlines, ratings, messaging, notifications, moderation, premium access, payments, marketplace, AI generation, Detective, Mafia, sabotage/economic/systems multiplayer, tournaments, and external asset APIs.
